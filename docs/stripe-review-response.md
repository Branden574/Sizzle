## Content moderation (this is what clears the review)

> Every piece of user content is moderated:
> 1. **Automated screening before it appears** — each uploaded video's thumbnail
>    and every image/caption pass automated text + vision moderation; anything
>    flagged is blocked from publishing.
> 2. **In-app reporting** — one tap on any post, comment, or profile opens a
>    5-category report flow into an internal moderation queue.
> 3. **Block & mute** — users can fully block or mute any account.
> 4. **Published contact point** — https://getsizzle.app/contact (also in Settings).
>
> We act on reported content within 24 hours — a commitment published in our
> Terms and shown to every reporter. (These are the same four mechanisms Apple
> required and approved under App Store Guideline 1.2 for user-generated content.)

## What customers pay for & how money flows

> Payments are for **food/recipe content and creator support only**, and happen
> on our website (getsizzle.app) — the iOS app contains no in-app purchases.
> Three revenue types, all tied to recipe content:
> - **Creator tips / support** — voluntary payments to a creator.
> - **Creator subscriptions** — optional monthly subscription to a creator.
> - **Premium recipe unlocks** — one-time purchase to unlock a specific recipe.
>
> We use **Stripe Connect (Express connected accounts)** to pay creators. Each
> purchase is a **destination charge**: after Stripe processing fees, funds split
> ~90% to the creator / ~10% platform fee (with small minimums), and the platform
> absorbs refund/chargeback loss protection so creators aren't clawed back.
>
> Creator payouts are **gated**: an account must reach 1,000 followers and
> 100,000 video views (server-enforced) before it can set up payouts and earn —
> so only established creators on the platform receive funds.

## Description (both stores)
```
Sizzle is the recipe app for people who love food videos — and actually want
to cook them.

WATCH IT
A full-screen feed of recipe videos from real home cooks. No ads, ever.

THEN COOK IT
Every video is a real recipe underneath: ingredients that scale to your
serving size, step-by-step cook mode with hands-free timers, and a shopping
list that builds itself.

WHAT'S IN YOUR KITCHEN?
Type the ingredients you have — "chicken, rice, gochujang" — and find recipes
you can make tonight.

MAKE IT YOURS
• Cook This: post your own take on any recipe — it starts pre-filled and
  credits the original
• Made-It Journal: log every cook with a photo, note, and rating
• Boards: curate public collections and share them anywhere
• Send recipes to friends as rich cards in DMs
• Follow the cooks you love; keep your account private if you prefer

FOR CREATORS
Honest analytics built around what matters: how many people actually cooked
your recipe — not just watched it.

Recipes, in motion. Come get hungry.
```
